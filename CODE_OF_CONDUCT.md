# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1][cc].

[cc]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## In short

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are disagreeing with is trying to
make the project better, and argue about the work rather than about them.

Unacceptable: harassment, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery,
publishing others' private information, sustained disruption, and the kind of
technically-accurate contempt that makes a project unpleasant to contribute to.

## Reporting

Report privately through
[GitHub's advisory form](https://github.com/rsh1k/revoco/security/advisories/new),
which is monitored by the maintainers and not public.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who cannot be impartial about a
report — including because it concerns them — recuse themselves from handling it.

## Enforcement

Consequences scale with severity and repetition: a private correction, a public
one, a temporary ban, or a permanent one. Maintainers who break this document
are held to it more firmly than contributors are, not less.
