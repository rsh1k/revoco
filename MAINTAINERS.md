Revoco has one maintainer. That is a fact about the project's age rather than a
policy, and it is the single largest thing standing between it and any software
foundation: both the ASF and the CNCF weigh how many legally independent people
can keep a project alive. Anyone landing sustained, reviewed work here will be
invited to this table.

## Becoming a maintainer

There is no quota and no waiting period. The bar is a track record: several
merged changes, review comments other people found useful, and judgement about
what *not* to change. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for where the
load-bearing invariants are, because the fastest way to demonstrate that
judgement is to notice one before breaking it.
